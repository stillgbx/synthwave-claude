# Changelog

## Unreleased

* The grid rolls again in the desktop app, and the stars twinkle. The desktop app draws still a scene holding a media query, but for reduced motion, so the scenes hold no other: on a window taller than wide, the scene is no longer brought in from the edges.
* The sun sits in the right margin, over lower mountains, where the conversation no longer hides it.
* The grid follows one true perspective: the rays meet at the vanishing point on the horizon and every cell stays square. Each line is drawn at its own depth, so the grid is whole even when it stands still.
* The scene keeps all its styles in its style sheet, none in style attributes.

## 0.1.0

* A port of the [SynthWave '84 VS Code theme](https://github.com/robb0wen/synthwave-vscode) by Robb Owen: its neon colors for the whole Claude desktop app (dark mode).
* A retro sunset behind the conversation: a striped sun, wireframe mountains, a neon grid rolling toward you, twinkling stars.
* `/synthwave84 scene | still | off` to switch.
